Alpha LPG: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Applies to the Alpha LPG customer app, rider app and admin app.

This explains what the Alpha LPG apps collect, why, and the choices you have. We only collect what we need to take and deliver your gas order.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number and name (and optionally email, business name and type).
  • Delivery addresses, including the map spot you choose.
  • Your orders: items, prices, payment method, transaction ID, amount, notes, rating and feedback.
  • Payment receipt pictures that you choose to send us for online payments.
  • Messages you send in the order chat with the shop and your rider.
  • A photo the rider takes at delivery, to show the cylinders arrived.
  • Your phone's location only when you tap "Use my current location" (customer app). We do not track customers in the background.
  • Riders: while on shift and during a delivery, the rider app shares the rider's location with the shop and the customer.
  • Basic crash reports (error text and app version) so we can fix problems.

Why we use it

To deliver your cylinders, contact you about an order, let your rider find you, confirm payments, send invoices, give support, prevent misuse, and keep business, tax and safety records.

Who can see it

Alpha LPG owner and staff, and the rider assigned to your order. The rider sees your name, phone number and address only while the order is open. During delivery you can see the rider's name, phone and live location. Your location is never shared with other customers. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Services we use

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and orders. Geoapify provides map tiles, address search and routes; it receives the map area or search text, not your name or phone number. WhatsApp, phone calls and Google Maps open only when you tap them.

Payments

The apps do not collect card numbers or wallet PINs. JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfers happen in those apps. We store the transaction ID, amount and receipt picture you send, only to check and match your payment.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account in the customer app: Account, then "Delete my account". This removes your sign-in, profile and saved locations. Past orders are kept without a way to sign in, for as long as we need them for business, tax and safety records. You can also ask us to delete or correct your data using the contact details below.

Children

The apps are for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
