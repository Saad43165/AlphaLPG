Delete your Alpha LPG account

In the app

  1. Open the Alpha LPG customer app.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This removes your sign-in, profile and saved delivery locations.

What we keep

Past orders (items, prices, delivery address) are kept without a way to sign in, for business, tax and safety records.

Cannot open the app?

Call 03085745740 or email info@alphalpg.pk from the phone number or email on your account and we will delete it for you.
